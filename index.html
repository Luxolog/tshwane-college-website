PRIVATE FUNDING & BURSARIES

Funding can come from the private sector.

Tshwane College is a private institution. We do not offer or accept NSFAS funding. Students may use private funding, employer sponsorships and corporate bursaries where the sponsoring organisation approves the arrangement.

MESSAGES FROM LEADERSHIP

Leadership with a clear purpose.

Hear directly from the people responsible for the academic journey, career pathways and long-term vision of Tshwane College.

01

HIGH SCHOOL & MATRIC RE-WRITE

A message from the Principal

Every learner deserves an environment where they can grow in confidence, strengthen their academic foundation and prepare for the opportunities ahead.

Our High School and Matric Re-Write pathway is about more than completing subjects. It is about giving learners a structured environment, focused support and a renewed opportunity to reach their academic goals.

We want our learners to leave us with discipline, confidence and the determination to take their next step — whether that is further study, skills development or entering the world of work.

Draft message for approval by the High School Principal.
02

TEVT • CAREER CAMPUS @ 228

A message from the Principal

Career-focused education should prepare people for the realities of work — with practical knowledge, relevant skills and the confidence to keep learning.

At Career Campus @ 228, our focus is on creating a learning environment where students can connect education with a career direction and develop capabilities that can be applied beyond the classroom.

Our goal is to help each student understand not only what they are studying, but where that learning can take them.

Draft message for approval by the TEVT Principal.
03

THE DIRECTOR

The journey. The dream. The vision.

“Tshwane College was built from a belief that education can change the direction of a person's life.”

The journey is about building an institution that remains relevant to the people it serves — an institution where academic education, technical skills, career preparation and personal development can meet.

The dream is bigger than buildings or qualifications. It is to see young people and adults discover their potential, gain confidence and create better futures for themselves, their families and their communities.

The vision is simple: to keep building an institution that prepares people for the future while remaining grounded in the values, discipline and purpose that brought us here.

Draft message for approval by the Director.
