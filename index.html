PRIVATE FUNDING & BURSARIES

Funding can come from the private sector.

Tshwane College is a private institution. We do not offer or accept NSFAS funding. Students may use private funding, employer sponsorships and corporate bursaries where the sponsoring organisation approves the arrangement.
