# Tshwane College Website Prototype

A mobile-first, accessible and search-ready prototype for a modern Tshwane College website.

## Product direction

The website is designed to become a **student recruitment and service platform**, not just an online brochure.

### Initial priorities
- Clear programme discovery and search
- Strong mobile-first application journey
- Career guidance / programme finder
- Funding and admissions guidance
- Student support and contact pathways
- Student stories, campus proof and news
- SEO foundations and structured content
- Accessibility and low-data performance
- PWA-ready foundation
- Clear separation between verified college information and prototype placeholders

## Before public launch

Replace all prototype content with information supplied and approved by Tshwane College:
- Official logo and brand guidelines
- Verified programme catalogue
- Accreditation / registration information
- Entry requirements and NQF levels
- Fees and payment information
- Application dates
- Campus addresses and contact details
- Approved application portal
- Student portal / LMS links
- NSFAS or other funding guidance
- Real campus/student photography
- Policies including POPIA, privacy, cookies and accessibility
- Social media links

## Recommended phase 2

1. Real online application integration
2. Application status lookup / student portal links
3. WhatsApp admissions integration
4. CRM lead capture and automated follow-up
5. Events/open-day registration
6. Student stories and graduate outcomes
7. Course comparison and career-matching tools
8. Analytics dashboard and conversion tracking
9. CMS for news, programmes and intake dates
10. Performance, security and accessibility testing

## Important

This repository is a prototype. No programme, accreditation, fee, contact detail or application claim should be treated as official until verified by the college.
